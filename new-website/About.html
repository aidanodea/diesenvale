Diesenvale is a family-run stainless steel fabricator in Charleville, Co. Cork. Founded in 1983 by Eddie Biggane with a single guillotine and two TIG welders, the shop now ships precision-engineered vessels and road tankers across Europe.

We don't sub-contract fabrication. Every weld is laid by a Diesenvale welder, every plate traced from mill to cert, every pressure test logged. We've shipped vessels to dairies in Connacht, breweries in Cork, pharma plants in Tipperary, distilleries in Wexford and chemical plants in Belgium. The same crew that welded silo #001 in 1983 trained the crew welding silo #1,047 today.
