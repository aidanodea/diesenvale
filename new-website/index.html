02How we work

SIX STEPS.
NO SHORTCUTS.

Every project follows the same six-stage protocol. We don't sub-out fabrication, we don't ship half-finished, and nothing leaves the workshop without being signed off.

STEP / 01
Brief
Sit-down or call. We translate ambition into a drawing.
STEP / 02
Design
3D model, stress calcs, P&ID review, fittings schedule.
STEP / 03
Procure
Plate, fittings, valves & instrumentation — fully traceable.
STEP / 04
Fabricate
Rolled, longitudinal & circumferential welds, X-ray inspected.
STEP / 05
Finish
Mechanical or electropolish, passivation, branding.
STEP / 06
Install
Crane-lift, pipe-up, CIP commissioning, sign-off.
